Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Regal Gambit and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then tap Delete account, then confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign in, and repeat.

Delete your account by email

If you cannot open the app, email dineshdxb9583@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account" and we will delete it.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, photo, rating, diamonds, referral code), your matchmaking entry and your finished online game records are deleted.

What is kept

Purchases remain with your Google Play account and are handled by Google. Ad-related data held by Google is governed by Google's policies.
