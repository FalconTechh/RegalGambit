1. Information we collect

a) Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password when you create an account with email, or your Google account name and email when you choose "Continue with Google". Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Profile details: the display name you choose and, optionally, a profile photo (resized and stored as a small image).
  • Reports and messages: if you report a player, the reason you give and related game details.
  • Invite codes: a referral code you enter or receive when joining through an invite link.

b) Information created when you play

  • Your rating, number of games, wins, losses and draws, rankings and weekly points.
  • Your diamond balance (the in-app currency) and rewards you have claimed.
  • Records of online games: the moves, players, result and time, so that games, history and anti-cheating checks work.
  • Your own invite code and who invited you (so invite rewards can be given).

c) Technical information

  • Your device model, app version and the time you were last active. This is kept in a private record that only you and the app administrator can read.
  • Your IP address and basic diagnostic data, which are processed automatically by Google services (Firebase, Google Play services and AdMob) when the App connects to the internet.
  • Your Android advertising ID, used by Google AdMob for ads (see section 4). You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings.

d) Purchase information

Purchases are made through Google Play. We receive confirmation of what you bought (for example, Regal Pass, Remove ads, or a diamond pack) and a purchase token. For diamond packs we also keep a receipt (purchase token, number of diamonds, time and your user ID) so that one purchase can only be credited once. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details.

e) Voice moves

If you use "Voice move", the App asks your phone's built-in speech recognition service (provided by Google or your phone maker) to turn your speech into text. The App does not record or store audio; it only receives the recognised text of your move. Your use of the speech service is governed by its provider's privacy policy.

f) Notifications

If you switch on daily reminders, the App shows notifications generated on your phone. You can turn them off in the App or in Android settings.

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To run online games, ratings, leaderboards, seasons and prizes.
  • To give and track diamonds, missions, achievements, invite rewards and purchases.
  • To show ads and, where you have agreed, personalised ads.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: detect cheating, handle reports, ban abusive accounts and prevent fraud.
  • To fix problems, improve the App and decide which features to build.
  • To meet legal duties and to respond to your requests.

Where the law of your country requires a legal basis (for example the EU or UK GDPR), we rely on: performing our contract with you (running your account and game), your consent (personalised ads, notifications), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, improving the App) and legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other players can see your public profile: display name, profile photo, rating, rank, game statistics, and what happens in games you play with them (moves, result).
  • Service providers who process data for us: Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore database hosting), Google Play (billing and installs), Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (ads and consent).
  • The app administrator (the developer, and any admins the developer appoints) can view player records to run the service, handle reports and give support.
  • Legal and safety: we may share information if required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights and safety of players or others.
  • Business changes: if the App is transferred to another owner, your information may move with it, under this policy.

4. Advertising and your consent

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including banner, native, full-screen, app-open and rewarded ads. AdMob may use your advertising ID and other data to choose and measure ads.

  • In regions where the law requires it (such as the EEA and UK) the App first shows Google's consent form so that you can accept or refuse personalised ads.
  • You can change your choice or reset your advertising ID in your phone: Settings → Google → Ads (path varies by phone).
  • Buying Remove ads or Regal Pass removes ads from the App.

More about how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Purchases and payments

All payments are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Subscriptions are managed (and can be cancelled) in the Google Play Store app. We only learn whether a purchase succeeded and what it was for.

6. Data stored on your device

Some information stays only on your phone and is not sent to us: your local game history and analysis, puzzle progress and streaks, Kingdom progress, daily missions and spin status, board and colour theme, sound settings and reminder settings. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Profile, rating, diamonds and game records are kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account (see section 8) your profile, rating, diamonds, invite code and finished game records are deleted.
  • Purchase receipts and records needed for security, fraud prevention, taxes or legal claims may be kept for as long as the law or a genuine need requires.
  • Copies in backups are removed on the normal backup cycle.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Edit your profile: change your display name and photo in Profile (each can be changed once every 30 days to prevent abuse).
  • Delete your account: in the App go to Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, rating, diamonds and finished game records. Purchases stay with your Google Play account.
  • Ask us: email dineshdxb9583@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, or to ask a question. We will reply within a reasonable time (normally within 30 days).
  • Ads: manage personalisation as described in section 4.
  • Notifications: switch off in the App or Android settings.

Depending on where you live (for example India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU/UK under GDPR) you may also have the right to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict certain processing, to data portability and to complain to your data protection authority. We will honour these rights as the law requires.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are under the age of majority in your country, please use online features only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, protected by access rules so that players can only change their own records. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process and store data on servers in countries other than yours, including India, the United States and the European Union. By using the App you understand that your information may be transferred there, with the protections provided by those providers.
